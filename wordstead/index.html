    Board setup

    1–30 per dimension. Creating a board clears all letters, claims, and points.

    Four words, one enclosure

    Choose Legal word placement, then Set up 7×7 example (resets this puzzle). Preview and commit CAR at (4, 4) horizontal; CAB at (4, 4) vertical; RAT at (4, 6) vertical; BAT at (6, 4) horizontal. Coordinates are 1-based; press Select cell after editing them.

    Shared corners are reused. Before BAT commits, the center (5, 5) shows a prospective ? with score 0. Committing BAT awards +1, for 4 placements and territory score 1. Try ZZZ or a conflicting letter: counts, tiles and claims stay unchanged.

    Provisional demonstration word list

    This hand-selected vocabulary is incomplete. A missing English word may be rejected. It is not an official Scrabble dictionary and uses no external service.

    How to test an enclosure

    Choose Freeform enclosure lab. It keeps a separate board and permits unrestricted letter toggling and manual claims for diagnostics. Switching back restores legal play without lab edits.

    Place letters in rows 2 and 4, columns 2–4. Add letters at row 3, columns 2 and 4. The empty center (row 3, column 3) is enclosed and earns one point when claimed. Claim again to verify +0.

    All four board edges give outside access. Only letters block orthogonal paths; empty cells cannot pass diagonally. Detection updates after every edit. Claims persist if you reopen a fence or cover claimed land with a letter.

    A + marks cells from the latest claim. Another claim or letter edit changes earlier claims to ✓. Nothing is saved on refresh.
