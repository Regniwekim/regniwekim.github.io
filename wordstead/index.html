    Board setup

    Seeds independently control letter order and farm layout. Density is the fraction of eligible interior cells (0 disables features). Default target 1 is temporary and unbalanced. Layouts may be unsolvable. New puzzles use these settings; restart repeats the active run.

    1–30 per dimension. Creating a board clears all letters, claims, and points.

    Development diagnostics: four words, one enclosure

    The scripted QA example switches to Rack-limited puzzle, resets its board to 7×7, sets target 1, and arranges a full standard bag for this sequence. It is not an ordinary seeded random puzzle. The unrestricted example remains in Legal word placement lab.

    In rack mode, place C, A, R at (4, 4–6), then commit CAR. Place A and B at (5–6, 4), then commit CAB. Place A and T at (5–6, 6), then commit RAT. Place A at (6, 5), then commit BAT. Coordinates are 1-based. Shared letters are reused automatically; no typing is needed. In the word lab, enter the full words instead.

    Shared corners are reused. Before BAT commits, the center (5, 5) shows a prospective ? with score 0. Committing BAT awards +1, for 4 placements and territory score 1. Try ZZZ or a conflicting letter: counts, tiles and claims stay unchanged.

    Development diagnostics: farm capture fixtures

    These separate 7×7 fixtures place one feature at (5, 5) and use the same full bag and CAR, CAB, RAT, BAT sequence above. The original rack fixture remains unchanged. Select the feature to inspect it. Before BAT commits it remains uncaptured and rewards stay inactive.

    Crop: +1 base and +3 bonus, score 4 and victory. Fertile: +1 base and +1 bonus, score 2 and victory. Livestock: score 1, rack expands from 7 to 8 using one extra bag tile. Building: score 1 and one free exchange credit. Its next successful exchange increases exchanges but keeps strokes at 4; the following exchange raises strokes to 5. Utility fixtures use target 20 to keep playing.

    Restart repeats the selected fixture. New puzzle restores ordinary seeded play. Credits do not bypass bag requirements; invalid moves and previews award nothing. Captured features retain their icon and receive + (new) or ✓ (older); ? is preview only.

    How to test an enclosure

    Choose Freeform enclosure lab. It keeps a separate board and permits unrestricted letter toggling and manual claims for diagnostics. Switching back restores legal play without lab edits.

    Place letters in rows 2 and 4, columns 2–4. Add letters at row 3, columns 2 and 4. The empty center (row 3, column 3) is enclosed and earns one point when claimed. Claim again to verify +0.

    All four board edges give outside access. Only letters block orthogonal paths; empty cells cannot pass diagonally. Detection updates after every edit. Claims persist if you reopen a fence or cover claimed land with a letter.

    A + marks cells from the latest claim. Another claim or letter edit changes earlier claims to ✓. Nothing is saved on refresh.
