How to Play

Build a farm, one word at a time.

  1. Build connected words from the letters in your rack.
  2. Your first word must cross the starting square, ☆.
  3. Your letters form fences around the land.
  4. Enclose territory to earn farm points.
  5. Capture farm features for bonus points, extra rack space or free exchanges. Feature cells cannot receive letters.
  6. Reach the farm-point target to complete the puzzle.
Tiles, blanks & exchanging

Tap a drafted tile to pick it up, then tap another cell to move it. Return selected tile sends it back to its original rack slot. Undo returns the last tile placed or moved; Clear returns the whole draft. Nothing is spent until you commit.

Choose an A–Z letter when selecting a blank (◇). Use Change blank letter to reassign it before committing. Its chosen letter becomes permanent after commitment.

Reuse matching letters already on the board for free. Every complete word formed in either direction must be accepted by the word list.

Return or clear the draft before exchanging. Choose Exchange tiles, select rack tiles, then confirm or cancel. The bag needs at least as many tiles as you exchange. An exchange uses one free credit if available; otherwise it adds one stroke.

Crops add 3 bonus points; fertile ground adds 1. Livestock adds a rack slot, up to nine. Buildings grant a free exchange credit. Every captured cell also earns one territory point. Board edges do not act as fences.

Daily puzzles & local saving

Daily Puzzle shares the same farm and letters for each date. Free Play lets you choose your own settings. Daily dates begin at 00:00 UTC using your device clock. Your active challenge keeps its date until you explicitly start today's challenge. Automatic targets are experimental; difficulty varies and completion is not guaranteed.

Daily and Free Play save separately in this browser/profile on this device. They are not synchronized or backed up. Clearing website data or browser eviction can remove saves and history. Labs and scripted fixtures are not saved.

    Development diagnostics: four words, one enclosure

    The scripted QA example uses Free Play's view with a 7×7 board, target 1, and a full standard bag arranged for this sequence. Your production Free Play save is kept; Resume Free Play returns to it. Daily Puzzle stays separate. The fixture uses the preserved hand-selected demo vocabulary for QA. New puzzle returns to ESDB. The unrestricted example remains in Legal word placement lab.

    In rack mode, place C, A, R at (4, 4–6), then commit CAR. Place A and B at (5–6, 4), then commit CAB. Place A and T at (5–6, 6), then commit RAT. Place A at (6, 5), then commit BAT. Coordinates are 1-based. Shared letters are reused automatically; no typing is needed. In the word lab, enter the full words instead.

    Shared corners are reused. Before BAT commits, the center (5, 5) shows a prospective ? with score 0. Committing BAT awards +1, for 4 placements and territory score 1. Try ZZZ or a conflicting letter: counts, tiles and claims stay unchanged.

    Development diagnostics: farm capture fixtures

    These separate 7×7 fixtures place one feature at (5, 5) and use the same full bag and CAR, CAB, RAT, BAT sequence above. The original rack fixture remains unchanged. Select the feature to inspect it. Before BAT commits it remains uncaptured and rewards stay inactive.

    Crop: +1 base and +3 bonus, score 4 and victory. Fertile: +1 base and +1 bonus, score 2 and victory. Livestock: score 1, rack expands from 7 to 8 using one extra bag tile. Building: score 1 and one free exchange credit. Its next successful exchange increases exchanges but keeps strokes at 4; the following exchange raises strokes to 5. Utility fixtures use target 20 to keep playing.

    Restart repeats the selected fixture. New puzzle restores ordinary seeded play. Credits do not bypass bag requirements; invalid moves and previews award nothing. Captured features retain their icon and receive + (new) or ✓ (older); ? is preview only.

    How to test an enclosure

    Choose Freeform enclosure lab. It keeps a separate board and permits unrestricted letter toggling and manual claims for diagnostics. Switching back restores legal play without lab edits.

    Place letters in rows 2 and 4, columns 2–4. Add letters at row 3, columns 2 and 4. The empty center (row 3, column 3) is enclosed and earns one point when claimed. Claim again to verify +0.

    All four board edges give outside access. Only letters block orthogonal paths; empty cells cannot pass diagonally. Detection updates after every edit. Claims persist if you reopen a fence or cover claimed land with a letter.

    A + marks cells from the latest claim. Another claim or letter edit changes earlier claims to ✓. Nothing is saved on refresh.
