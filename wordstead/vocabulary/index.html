American English, size 60, variant level 1. Version esdb-en-us-60-1e5b7d3-ws1 contains 78,626 words. This is an English spelling word list, not an official Scrabble dictionary.

Which words count?

Input is case-insensitive; surrounding whitespace is ignored. Complete words must contain 2–30 ASCII A–Z letters. Spaces within words, hyphens, apostrophes, punctuation, digits and accented or unsupported glyphs are excluded. We do not remove punctuation or accents to create playable spellings.

The export excludes tagged names, abbreviations, nonwords, word parts and special categories. Spellings containing capital letters in the source are also excluded. Ordinary plurals, verb forms and other inflections remain when listed. Every complete main word and cross-word must be in this list. Blanks supply a letter and still require valid words.

Known limits

A spell-checking list differs from a word-game dictionary. Some obscure words and regional variants remain; familiar game words may be absent. Name and abbreviation metadata is incomplete. A spelling that is also an ordinary lowercase word can remain. Offensive, vulgar and dated terms may occur: no additional content filter or manual additions are applied.

Development QA examples use the preserved hand-selected demo vocabulary and label it on screen. Creating a new puzzle restores ESDB. A run keeps its vocabulary throughout play. Future vocabulary updates can change old puzzle solutions.
